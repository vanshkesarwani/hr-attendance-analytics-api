## Items Looked At and Decided Were NOT Defects

1. **PyMongo timezone awareness**: `client = MongoClient(..., tz_aware=True)`. While default PyMongo returns naive UTC datetimes, handling datetime timezones explicitly at application boundaries (converting naive UTC to aware UTC before formatting) is standard practice and not an intrinsic PyMongo bug.
2. **Presence statuses vs Full status enum**: The presence statuses `PRESENT`, `WFH`, and `ON_DUTY` are accepted by `POST /attendance/punch-in`, while `ABSENT` and `LEAVE` are only created via seeded back-office data or manual correction (`PATCH`). Having `Status` encompass all 5 values while `PresenceStatus` restricts punch-in to 3 is by design according to the business domain.
